# Add or replace a photo

Photos are part of the website code, so this goes through a preview you approve.

## Before you start

**Check you are allowed to use it.** Your own photos are fine. A photo from the
internet usually is not. Free, properly licensed photos: [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)
or [Pexels](https://pexels.com) — several of the current site photos came from
Unsplash.

**Do not resize or compress it yourself.** Send the original at full size. The
website shrinks and converts it automatically, for every screen size, better than
a manual export would.

**Name it usefully.** `hall-immeuble-montmartre.jpg` beats `IMG_4821.jpg` — you
will want to find it again in a year.

## Steps

1. Put the photo somewhere your AI can read it — the project folder on your
   computer is simplest.

2. Tell your AI where it goes, as precisely as you can:

   > I have added `hall-immeuble-montmartre.jpg` to the project folder. Please use
   > it as the second photo in the slider at the top of the Nooklean homepage,
   > replacing the current one.

3. Describe what is in the photo, in one sentence:

   > It shows a freshly cleaned entrance hall with a tiled floor.

   This becomes the alternative text — read aloud by screen readers, shown if the
   image fails to load, and read by Google. It is not optional, and you are the
   only one who knows what the photo shows.

4. Check the preview link, on your phone as well as your computer. Photos are
   cropped differently on narrow screens, and a face or a detail can be cut off.

5. Merge if it looks right.

## Replacing versus adding

**Replacing** is simpler — say which photo to swap, and the old one gets removed.

**Adding** to a slider or gallery means saying where in the order it goes: "after
the one with the staircase".

## If the site feels slower afterwards

Tell your AI. Usually the new photo is not being optimised because it was added
in a way that bypasses the automatic handling. It is a quick fix, but it needs
someone to notice — so mention it rather than living with it.

## Photos of people

If a photo shows an identifiable person — a colleague, a client, someone's pet in
their home — get their agreement first. For a small local business, a misused
photo is a real and avoidable problem.

## Later: uploading photos yourself

Right now photos go through a pull request. Uploading them directly from the back
office is possible and was deliberately left for later
(see `docs/decisions.md`, *Deliberately deferred*), because photos change rarely.
If you find yourself changing them weekly, say so — that is the signal to build it.
