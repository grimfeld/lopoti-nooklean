# Change a service's name or description

**Do this in the back office.** It takes effect immediately, with no deploy and
no pull request.

## Steps

1. Open the back office and log in.
2. Switch to the right business with the buttons at the top — **Lopoti** or
   **Nooklean**.
3. Go to **Services**.
4. Edit the text directly in the fields.
5. Click **Enregistrer**.

Reload the public website to see the change.

## What each field does

| Field | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Icône** | The small picture beside the service on the website |
| **Nom** | The service's title, on the website and in the dropdown on the form |
| **Description** | The sentence underneath the title |

Keep the name short — it has to fit on a card and inside a dropdown. The
description is where the detail goes.

## Choosing an icon

Click the icon field and you get a set of suggestions. You can also type or paste
any emoji:

- **Windows:** press <kbd>Windows</kbd> + <kbd>.</kbd>
- **Mac:** press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Space</kbd>

## Which animals a service is offered for (Lopoti only)

Under each animal on the **Services** page is a row of service chips. Click one
to turn it on or off for that animal.

This is what makes the form conditional: a visitor picks their animal first, and
only sees the services you have enabled for it. A visitor with a goldfish should
not be offered dog walking.

## Renaming a service that customers already booked

Safe. Existing requests keep the service name they were submitted with, so your
records stay true to what the customer actually asked for. Only the website and
the form change.

This does mean your request list can show both the old and the new name for a
while. That is correct, not a bug.

## What you cannot change here

The **words on the rest of the page** — headings, the homepage text, the bits
explaining how it works — are in the code, so they go through a preview first.
See [`edit-page-text.md`](edit-page-text.md).

The reason for the split: service lists are structured data that can be checked
automatically. Prose cannot, so it gets a preview and your approval instead.
