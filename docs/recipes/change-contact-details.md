# Change the contact email or phone number

Contact details appear in several places on both sites, so this is one change
with a few landing spots.

## Steps

1. Open the back office and log in.
2. Go to **Réglages** → **Coordonnées**.
3. Edit the email address, phone number and service area.
4. Click **Enregistrer**.

This covers the footer, the contact sections and the addresses used when you send
a quote. Reload the public sites to check.

## Per-business, not shared

Each business has its own contact details. Switch brands at the top and edit the
other one too if both are changing.

## If an old address is still showing somewhere

Some contact details may be written into a page's text rather than stored as a
setting — for example inside a sentence like "écrivez-nous à bonjour@nooklean.fr".
Those are part of the copy, so they go through a preview.

Tell your AI exactly what you saw and where:

> The footer shows the new email address but the "Nous contacter" paragraph on the
> cleaning site still shows the old one: bonjour@nooklean.fr. Please fix it.

## Changing the email address you actually receive mail at

Two separate things:

- **What the website shows** — the steps above.
- **Where the mail goes** — your email provider, nothing to do with the website.

Change the website only after the new mailbox works, or you will advertise an
address that bounces. Test it by sending yourself a message first.

## Quotes

Quotes are sent from your own mail program, so they come from whatever address it
is set up with — not from the setting above. If you change your email provider,
check that your mail program is sending from the right address before you send
the next quote.

## Phone number format

Write it as a French reader expects: `06 12 34 56 78`. On a phone, the website
turns it into a tappable link automatically, so there is no need to add `+33` or
anything else.
